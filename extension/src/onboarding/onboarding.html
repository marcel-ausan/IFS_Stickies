Notes that live on the record.

Pin a note to a customer order, a work order, a part — anything with its own page. Everyone on your team who opens that record sees it, and every note is stored in your own IFS environment. No server, no third party, nothing to host.

Your first note, in four steps

Two of these are once-only. After that, writing a note is one click.

1

Pin the extension

Click the puzzle-piece icon in Chrome’s toolbar and pin IFS Sticky Notes, so the popup is always one click away. You need it in the next step and rarely after that.

2

Allow it on your IFS site

The extension installs with no access to any website at all, so it needs permission for your IFS address — and nothing else.

  • Open your IFS Cloud in a tab and sign in as usual.
  • Click the extension icon. It recognises the site you are on.
  • Press Enable on this site and accept Chrome’s prompt.

No typing, and no need to reload — the notes appear on the tab you are already looking at.

    Not enabled anywhere yet.

    Chrome will say “read and change your data”. That is the only permission which lets any extension draw something on a page, and Chrome words it the same for every one of them.

    Nothing leaves your IFS environment. Notes are written to your own tenant, people are looked up in your own IFS person list, and there is no analytics, no telemetry and no other network call.

    3

    Open a record and write

    Notes appear on record pages — a single customer order, a work order — not on lobbies or list pages. Click the floating 📝 button, then +.

    • It saves as you type. There is no Save button.
    • Drag the note by its top bar. Drag the 📝 button itself to move it aside.
    • Eight colours, from the dots on the note’s top bar.
    4

    Mention someone, and they hear about it

    Type @ and part of a name or user id — the search matches both. Picking somebody does not send anything yet.

    A ✉ Notify button appears at the bottom of the note. Press it once the note actually says what you mean, and everyone newly tagged gets an e-mail linking straight back to this record. Delete an @token before pressing it and that person is quietly un-tagged.

    One thing your IFS administrator does first

    Notes are stored in IFS, so the environment needs somewhere to put them — two small configuration packages, imported once in a set order. Usually not the same person who installs the extension.

    Open the administrator setup

    That page has both packages to download, the steps, and a Copy as text button for pasting into a ticket. Until it is done notes will not save — the popup’s Check IFS setup button says so plainly rather than failing silently.

    Reopen this page any time from the extension popup. Access can be removed per site, and notes already written stay in IFS.
